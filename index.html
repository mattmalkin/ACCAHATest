Clinical Definitions & Criteria

Definition of Moderate or Greater Ischemia

  • Nuclear (SPECT/PET): ≥10% myocardium ischemic
  • Echo: ≥3/16 segments with stress-induced severe hypokinesis/akinesis or WMSI >1.1
  • CMR: ≥12% myocardium ischemic and/or wall motion abnormalities matching Echo criteria

HF Severity Classification

  • Low/Moderate HFrEF: NYHA I-III, EF >25%, GFR >40, Euvolemia
  • High HFrEF: NYHA IIIb-IV, EF <25%, GFR <30, Fluid overload
  • Low/Moderate HFpEF: NYHA I-III, EF >40%, BP controlled
  • High HFpEF: EF >40%, poorly controlled BP, RVSP >50mmHg, fluid overload

Stages of Valvular Heart Disease

  • Stage A: At risk (no structural disease/symptoms)
  • Stage B: Progressive (mild-moderate, asymptomatic)
  • Stage C: Asymptomatic severe (compensated)
  • Stage D: Symptomatic severe
